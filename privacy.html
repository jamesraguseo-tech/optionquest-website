Privacy Policy for OptionQuest

Last Updated: July 25, 2026

OptionQuest Enterprises ("we", "us", or "our") operates the OptionQuest Mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

Summary:

We do not collect any personal identity information. Your options simulation history and game progression remain stored securely on your own device.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or device contacts). All educational simulation data, transaction histories, QuestCoins balances, and completed course achievements are kept strictly on your local device storage.

2. Third-Party Services and Analytics

To ensure application stability, monitor app performance, and run optional educational features, we utilize third-party services that process anonymous usage data. All analytics data collection is fully optional, and you can opt-out at any time by turning off the "Share Analytics Data" setting in your Profile drawer inside the mobile application.

  • Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymous aggregate behavior data (e.g., lessons clicked, simulated trades executed) to help us understand which features are most popular.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collects anonymous diagnostic logs when the application crashes to help us debug and resolve runtime issues.
  • Google AdMob: Displays advertising units within the application. AdMob may utilize device identifiers (like the advertising ID) to serve relevant ad units.

3. Children's Privacy

Our Service is intended for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you find that your child has provided us with any data, please contact us at the address below so we can verify and delete it.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any modifications by publishing the updated text on this page. You are advised to review this page occasionally for any changes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
